# Service Level Agreement Policy

Northgate Digital Ltd. Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2025.

## Ticket priorities and targets

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Service down for the whole site (no phones, no internet, server down) | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Service badly affected for many users | 30 minutes | 8 hours |
| P3 | One user or one service affected | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| P4 | Request or question | 1 working day | 72 hours |

Targets are measured in clock hours from the moment the ticket is logged. The clock does not stop outside office hours for P1 and P2.

## Escalation

When a ticket has used 75% of its resolution target the Service Desk Lead is alerted and must either escalate it to second line or update the customer with a new estimate. A P1 that reaches its target is escalated to the Technical Director.

## Hosting and data centre availability

Cloud servers and colocation are covered by a monthly uptime target of 99.9%.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime for a server falls below 99.9%, the customer is entitled to a service credit of 5% of that server's monthly fee for each full 0.1% below target, up to a maximum of 50%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days and are applied to the next invoice. Credits are not given for planned maintenance announced at least 5 working days in advance.
