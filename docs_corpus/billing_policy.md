# Billing and Credit Control Policy

Northgate Digital Ltd (fictional). Version 2.0.

## Invoicing

Recurring services are invoiced monthly in arrears on the last day of the month. One-off work (software projects, hardware) is invoiced on delivery, or in stages for projects over £10,000.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

## Reminders

1. 7 days overdue: automatic reminder email.
2. 30 days overdue: second reminder from Finance, with a phone call to the customer's finance contact.
3. 60 days overdue: Finance may place the account on credit hold (no new orders or hardware) and must tell the account manager.
4. 90 days overdue: referral to the Finance Director for a decision on suspension of non-essential services or debt recovery.

Essential services (phone lines used for emergency calls, healthcare customers' systems) are never suspended without Director approval.

## Disputes

If a customer disputes an invoice, reminders for that invoice are paused and the account manager must resolve the dispute within 14 days.
