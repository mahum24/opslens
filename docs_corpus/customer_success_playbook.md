# Customer Success Playbook

Northgate Digital Ltd. For account managers.

## Who to call first

Each week, work through customers in the High churn-risk band, starting with those paying the most per month. The churn model lists up to three reasons for each customer's risk; use them to prepare.

## Review call checklist

1. Read the last 90 days of tickets, especially P1s and SLA breaches.
2. Check for overdue invoices; a billing dispute is often the real complaint.
3. Ask the customer how things are going before raising problems.
4. Agree up to three actions with dates, and log them as tasks.
5. Book a follow-up within 30 days.

## Common reasons and what helps

- **Repeated outages or SLA breaches:** offer a technical review with second line support and, where the SLA allows, a service credit. Make sure the root cause is fixed, not just the ticket closed.
- **Low satisfaction scores:** ask which tickets went badly; agree a named engineer for the next month.
- **Falling call volume:** the customer may be moving to Teams or mobile; offer Teams Phone integration rather than lose the account.
- **Paying late:** check for a dispute first; involve Finance before the 60-day credit hold.
- **Uses only one service:** customers with one service leave more easily; a bundle review (e.g. adding backup to hosting) can help, but only once current problems are solved.

## Retention offers

Account managers may offer up to 10% off for 3 months without approval. Larger offers need the Commercial Director.
